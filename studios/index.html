A designer hands in their notice and takes eleven years of “oh, they always want it in Pantone” with them. Not the files — those you have. The other stuff.

Things it can't do.

Every other page you've read today told you what its software does. Here's the other list, because you'd find out in week two anyway and week two is a rotten time to find out.

  • Support is email, not a phone line.One business day, Monday to Friday — written down here rather than discovered by you in month three. No weekends, no 2am pager. If you need somebody on call through the night, we’re the wrong shop and we’d rather say so now than take your money and disappoint you later.
  • You’d be early.There’s no wall of client logos on this page because there is no wall of client logos. That’s exactly why the founding price is what it is. Open the demo and judge the software instead of the logos — nothing is behind a “book a call” wall.
  • Your clients can’t pay by card in it.Invoices get raised, sent and chased, and your client sees exactly what’s outstanding. The actual paying still happens wherever you take payment today.
  • The handover only knows what’s in the system.It reconstructs a leaver’s job from your clients, projects, tasks and pitches — which is genuinely most of it, and it’s assembled before you ask them a single question. What it can’t see is whatever never got typed anywhere: the supplier who only answers WhatsApp, the reason something is priced oddly. That’s exactly what the exit questions are for, and it tells the new person when something wasn’t captured rather than making it up.
  • The automations don’t run.There’s a rather lovely workflow builder in there. It draws beautiful diagrams. Nothing executes them yet. Please don’t buy this for that.
  • Single sign-on works, but you supply the keys.Sign in with Google, Microsoft or GitHub is built and tested. Switching it on needs credentials from your own Google or Microsoft account — a twenty minute job, and one we’ll do with you on the call rather than leave you to it. Until then it’s email and password, with sessions that expire and a proper audit trail.
