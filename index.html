Fifty-three screens

Their half, and your half.

These are the ones that change your week. The rest are there when you want them and quiet when you don't.

Their side — what the client sees
  • 01

    Clients get their own front door

    They sign in and see their projects, their files, their invoices — and absolutely nothing belonging to anyone else. You choose what each client can see.

  • 02

    Decisions with a name on them

    Work goes out as a decision, not an attachment. Approve, or request changes. Recorded, dated, signed by a human. “I never approved that” stops being a conversation.

  • 03

    Four roles, so the right person decides

    Account owner signs and pays. Manager approves. Team member uploads and comments. Viewer looks. Set per client in about a minute, and the wrong person simply doesn’t get the button.

Your side — what you get
  • 01

    Everything live, and whose turn it is

    Projects, tasks, calendar and the pipeline in one place. At a glance: what’s moving, what’s stuck, and who it’s sitting with. Usually them.

  • 02

    Invoices that know what they’re for

    Raise it against the work, send it, and see who hasn’t paid without opening anything else. It bills — it isn’t your accountant, and it won’t pretend to be.

  • 03

    Your name on it. Not ours.

    Your logo, your colours, your domain. When your client signs in, nothing on the screen mentions us. As far as they know, you built it. We’re fine with that.

  • 04

    Your data leaves whenever you like

    Backed up nightly, and the backup is read back to check it actually restores — an unread backup is a hope, not a backup. Any client’s records export to a plain file on request. Nothing about this holds you hostage.

What it doesn't do.

Every one of these is on the front page rather than discovered by you in month three. If one is a deal-breaker, better we both find out now.

  • Support is email, not a phone line.

    One business day, Monday to Friday — written down here rather than discovered by you in month three. No weekends, no 2am pager. If you need somebody on call through the night, we’re the wrong shop and we’d rather say so now than take your money and disappoint you later.

  • You’d be early.

    There’s no wall of client logos on this page because there is no wall of client logos. That’s exactly why the founding price is what it is. Open the demo and judge the software instead of the logos — nothing is behind a “book a call” wall.

  • Your clients can’t pay by card in it.

    Invoices get raised, sent and chased, and your client sees exactly what’s outstanding. The actual paying still happens wherever you take payment today.

  • The automations don’t run.

    There’s a rather lovely workflow builder in there. It draws beautiful diagrams. Nothing executes them yet. Please don’t buy this for that.

  • Single sign-on works, but you supply the keys.

    Sign in with Google, Microsoft or GitHub is built and tested. Switching it on needs credentials from your own Google or Microsoft account — a twenty minute job, and one we’ll do with you on the call rather than leave you to it. Until then it’s email and password, with sessions that expire and a proper audit trail.
