The part that matters

Why “I never approved that” stops working.

01

The log refuses to be edited

Every action is written append-only and hash-chained: each record’s hash covers its content and links to the one before it. The database rejects updates and deletes outright, and one button checks the whole chain.

02

A signature captures the document, not a tick

Signing records the name, the moment, the IP address and a hash of that exact version. Change a word afterwards and the hash no longer matches what was signed.

03

Approvals supersede, they never overwrite

When something is re-agreed, the new approval points back at the one it replaces and both stay. The question at final account becomes “show me the sequence”, and it is there with a name and a price on each step.
